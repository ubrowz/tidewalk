Tidewalk

Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026

Tidewalk does not collect any data. Your steps and your reef stay on your iPhone.

What the app reads

To keep your reef going, Tidewalk reads your step count: daily totals for the last two weeks and today's steps per hour. It reads them from Apple Health (which includes steps from your iPhone, an Apple Watch, or another watch that syncs with Health), only after you allow it. If Health has no steps for the app, it uses the iPhone's own step count (Motion & Fitness) instead, again only with your permission.

Tidewalk only reads step counts. It does not read any other health data, and it never writes anything to Apple Health.

What the app keeps, and where

The app keeps on your iPhone, inside the app itself:

  • your daily step totals (at most the last 70 days), used to work out the water level of your reef;
  • the state of your reef (the fish, the water level);
  • your settings (daily step goal, sound on or off).

None of this leaves your iPhone. When you delete the app, all of it is deleted.

What the app does not do

  • It does not send any data anywhere. The app makes no network connections at all.
  • It has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • It does not share or sell data, and it does not use your data for advertising or marketing.
  • It does not store health data in iCloud.

Your choices

You can stop the app reading your steps at any time: in the iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Tidewalk, and for Motion & Fitness in Settings → Privacy & Security → Motion & Fitness. Without steps, the reef simply stays as it is.

Children

Tidewalk collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Not medical advice

Tidewalk is a motivational game. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.

Changes
