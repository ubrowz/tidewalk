Tidewalk

Privacy Policy

Effective 6 November 2026

Tidewalk does not collect any data. How much you move, and your reef, stay on your iPhone.

What the app reads

To keep your reef going, Tidewalk reads how much you move from Apple Health, only after you allow it:

  • step count;
  • wheelchair pushes (push count);
  • active energy;
  • exercise minutes.

It reads daily totals for the last 30 days, and today's totals per hour. Health includes data from your iPhone, an Apple Watch, or another watch that syncs with Health. You can allow some of these and not others. If Health has no data for the app, Tidewalk uses the iPhone's own step count (Motion & Fitness) instead, again only with your permission.

Versions up to 1.4 read the step count only. The other three are read from version 1.5 on, which is being tested now.

Tidewalk does not read any other health data, and it never writes anything to Apple Health.

What the app keeps, and where

The app keeps on your iPhone, inside the app itself:

  • the water level of each day (at most the last 70 days), worked out from how much you moved;
  • how much movement means high tide for you, which Tidewalk fits to you over time;
  • if you answer the question “How active did today feel?”: your answer with that day's totals, at most one a day, for the last year (your activity log);
  • the state of your reef (the fish, the water level);
  • your settings (your own goal if you set one, reminders, sound on or off).

None of this leaves your iPhone. When you delete the app, all of it is deleted.

What the app does not do

  • It does not send any data anywhere. The app makes no network connections at all.
  • It has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • It does not share or sell data, and it does not use your data for advertising or marketing.
  • It does not store health data in iCloud.

Your activity log

In the app's settings, “Share my activity log” makes a file with your answers and that day's totals, and opens the iPhone's share sheet. You choose where it goes, for example an email or AirDrop. Nothing is sent unless you send it yourself. If you send it to the developer, it is only used to learn how people experience their movement compared with what the phone measures, and to improve Tidewalk. It is not shared with anyone, and it is deleted when you ask.

Pictures of your reef

“Share a picture of your reef” makes a picture of the reef on your iPhone and opens the share sheet. If you choose “Save Image”, the iPhone asks to let Tidewalk add photos. Tidewalk can then only add the picture to your photo library. It never sees or reads your photos.

Emails you send

The app has a “Suggest a fish” link. It opens your own email app with a new message to the developer, and nothing is sent unless you send it yourself. Suggestions and other emails are only read to answer them and to choose new fish. They are not shared with anyone.

Your choices

You can stop the app reading any of these at any time: in the iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Tidewalk, and for Motion & Fitness in Settings → Privacy & Security → Motion & Fitness. Without them, the reef simply stays as it is.

Children

Tidewalk collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Not medical advice

Tidewalk is a motivational game. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.
