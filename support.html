How it works

  • Today: every morning the tide is out and the water is hazy. Each step raises it and clears it; at your goal it is high tide with clear water.
  • Good days: reaching your goal brings gifts: a feeding, a visitor (a sea turtle, an eagle ray or a barracuda), and a youngster for one of the reef's couples.
  • Weeks: as good days add up, new species move in. Fish pair up, raise young and live about two weeks, so the reef has generations. A missed day costs nothing.

Common questions

The app doesn't show my steps

Check that Tidewalk may read your steps: Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Tidewalk, and turn on Steps. Steps from a watch only appear once the watch has synced with the Health app.

My watch isn't an Apple Watch

Tidewalk reads steps from Apple Health. Most watches (for example Garmin) can sync their steps to Apple Health in their own app's settings. Without any steps in Health, Tidewalk uses the iPhone's own step count.

How do I change my daily step goal?

Tap the water drop in the top corner of the screen. The sheet that opens shows today's steps and lets you change your goal.

How do I turn the sound off?

Tap the speaker next to the water drop. The sound also follows your iPhone's silent switch.

Can I play with the fish?

Tap a fish to bring it to the front of the tank. Double-tap anywhere to knock on the glass: the fish dart away for a moment, and the clownfish hide in their anemone.

Why does the reef turn with my phone?

The water stays level, like in a real tank, so the reef turns when you tilt your iPhone.

Does Tidewalk collect my data?

No. Your steps and your reef stay on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
